# In Postcards We Meet

**In Postcards We Meet** is a web-based visual archive built from a collection of postcards.

The project was originally created under the title **"712 Postcards" (《712张明信片》)** and was first presented publicly at the **2026 Central Academy of Fine Arts Undergraduate Graduation Exhibition** as a graduation project by a student of the School of Design.

After the graduation exhibition, the project was further developed into an open website. By making the website and its source code publicly accessible, I hope to extend the connections established by these postcards into a wider world.

---

## About the Project

Postcards are a simple yet symbolic form of communication. In this efficiency-obsessed era, they endow images and words with materiality, making each one unique through the processes of writing, transporting, wearing, and reading.

Over the past six years, I have received **712 postcards from strangers around the world** through an international postcard exchange website. As tangible visual materials, they have allowed me to connect with an invisible world through imagination.

Four dimensions intersect on a single postcard: **printed images, handwritten text, connected spaces, and transcended time**. When I receive a postcard, all these elements manifest simultaneously, becoming the entire source material for this creation.

Each postcard carries a fleeting moment of the sender's life. Its textual and visual, material and emotional elements are cut, reorganized, and woven into an interactive website telling stories about postcards and their senders.

Visitors navigate through **26 fictional scripts** collaged from original messages, a global space spanning reality and virtuality, and an archive of **712 images**.

The website's typeface is a collaborative creation in which each character is derived from the handwriting of different people. Corrections, circles, crossings-out, and even seemingly meaningless marks are preserved within the letterforms. In an age where technology increasingly blurs the boundaries between reality and virtuality, the typeface returns to the lived experience of a person lingering carefully over a page or expressing genuine emotion while writing.

Visitors can write a message using the virtual handwriting of strangers from around the world and send it to an email address, completing a virtual postcard delivery experience.

This creation responds to the tendency of visual communication to evolve toward comprehensive design through cross-media expression. Through 712 postcards, I share a flat yet profound network of space and time with senders far away. Although every individual and every object is independent and unique, we will eventually connect in a fleeting moment.

## Features

- An interactive archive of 712 postcards
- 26 fictional scripts constructed from original postcard messages
- A navigable space connecting physical and virtual geographies
- A visual archive organized around postcard images
- A typeface created from the handwriting of different postcard senders
- Interactive writing using virtual handwriting
- A virtual postcard sending experience
- A cross-media approach combining typography, image, text, space, and interaction
